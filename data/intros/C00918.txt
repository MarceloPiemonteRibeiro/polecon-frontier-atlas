In Switzerland, citizenship applications of immi- grants are decided by the municipality in which the im- migrant resides. Municipalities use two main types of regimes to vote on naturalization applications: direct democracy, in which citizens vote on the applications us- ing referendums, and representative democracy, in which elected legislators vote on the applications in the munici- pality council.
